01Our approach

StationQC is built to work without an account and with as little personal information as possible. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

02What the app uses

With your permission, your approximate or precise location while you use the app, to show nearby stations. If you turn on Scout Mode and allow “Always” access, your location is also checked in the background. Your preferences (fuel type, region, map style, saved and watched stations, saved and recent places, fuel consumption and alert settings) are saved on your device, and shared with the StationQC widgets on that device.

03How location is used

To list and sort stations near you, to power the widgets, Siri shortcuts and alerts, and to start and follow trips from your position. With Scout Mode and “Always” access, StationQC checks your location in the background as you drive, only to compare nearby prices and notify you of a deal. StationQC does not collect your location and has no servers of its own to send it to. You can change or withdraw this permission anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

04Price alerts

Price alerts are optional. When you turn them on, StationQC checks prices in the background for the stations you watch, and shows notifications on your device. Scout Mode, which is also optional, looks for deals near you and uses your location only if you have allowed it. Your alert settings and watched stations stay on your device. You can mute a station from its notification, and turn alerts off in the app or in Settings → Notifications.

05Third-party services

Fuel prices and price trends are retrieved from Gas Québec. Maps, place search and routes use Apple Maps services, the weather shown for a station comes from Apple Weather and is requested for the station’s location, and Siri and Shortcuts are provided by Apple. Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

06Beta sign-up on our website

If you ask to join the StationQC beta on our website, we collect your full name, your email address and the language you were reading in. We use them only to invite you to test StationQC through Apple TestFlight and to contact you about the beta. The form is processed by Formspree, our form provider, which delivers your request to our email, and your details are shared with Apple only when we add you as a tester in TestFlight. We do not sell them. To have them deleted, reply to any beta email we send you.

07What we do not do

StationQC has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. The beta form on our website is the only place we ask for your name or email. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

08Your control

You can clear recent places, saved places, saved stations and watched stations in the app, and delete the app to remove its data from your device. Because StationQC does not hold an account for you, your information stays under your control on your device.

09Children

StationQC is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10Your rights and changes

If you live in Québec or elsewhere in Canada, you have rights over your personal information under applicable privacy laws. We may update this policy; the date above will change accordingly.
