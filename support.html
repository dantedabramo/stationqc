How can we help?

A question about prices, location or widgets? Here are the most common fixes.

I cannot see nearby stations

  1. Check your Internet connection.
  2. In Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, allow StationQC.
  3. Outside Québec? Choose a Québec region in the app.

A fuel price looks wrong

  1. The price displayed at the pump is the final reference.
  2. Reopen the app with an Internet connection to refresh the data.
  3. Prices come from Gas Québec and can change between updates.

I am not getting price alerts

  1. In Settings → Notifications, allow StationQC, then check that alerts are on in the app.
  2. Turn on Background App Refresh for StationQC so prices can be checked while the app is closed.
  3. For Scout Mode deals while you drive, set location access to “Always”.
  4. Quiet hours hold alerts back, and each station only alerts once every few hours.

My widget is not updating

  1. Open StationQC to check your region and fuel preference.
  2. Check your Internet connection and location permission.
  3. iOS controls how often widgets refresh, so updates are not instant.

Siri cannot find my station

  1. Open StationQC first and check your preferences.
  2. Make sure Siri is enabled on your iPhone.
  3. Try a StationQC shortcut in the Shortcuts app.

The app says I am offline

  1. Connect to Wi-Fi or cellular data and reopen the app.
  2. Until then, the last prices available are shown and may be out of date.
  3. Routes and place search need a connection.
